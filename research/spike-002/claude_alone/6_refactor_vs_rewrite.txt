Refactor in place. With no schedule slack and a major feature landing in that exact area next quarter, a rewrite means you're shipping the feature on top of code that's never been in production — and you lose the implicit knowledge those 3 years of patches encode (the weird edge cases, the customer-specific workarounds, the "why is this here" lines that turn out to matter). Do the refactor as a series of small, behavior-preserving changes ahead of and alongside the feature work, guided by where the feature actually needs to touch — that way the cleanup is paid for by work you're doing anyway, and you can stop at any point without a half-finished rewrite blocking the release.
