Look first at the **shared currency/money formatting and aggregation layer**, not the 12 dashboards.

Specifically:

1. Find the commit or helper that changed rounding from **banker’s rounding / round half to even** to **standard half-up rounding**.
   Search for things like:
   - `round`
   - `toFixed`
   - `Decimal`
   - `ROUND_HALF_UP`
   - `ROUND_HALF_EVEN`
   - `bankers`
   - `currency`
   - `money`
   - `invoice total`
   - `payout amount`

2. Check whether rounding is happening **too early**.
   A common failure mode is:

   ```text
   round(line item) + round(line item) + round(line item)
   ```

   instead of:

   ```text
   round(sum(all exact line items))
   ```

   That will create penny drift across dashboards, payouts, and invoices.

3. Compare the app’s rounding mode against the **finance system’s contract**.
   If finance uses banker’s rounding, your app must match it exactly for:
   - invoice totals
   - tax calculations
   - payouts
   - refunds
   - dashboard aggregates
   - exported reports

4. Add regression tests around known `.005` cases.
   These are the danger cases:

   ```text
   1.005
   2.225
   10.235
   100.125
   ```

   Test both individual amounts and summed totals.

The immediate fix is probably to restore the finance-system rounding mode in the canonical money calculation path, then make dashboards consume those calculated values instead of re-rounding independently. The dashboards are symptoms; the source of truth is the money math boundary.
