Good starting point. Before picking a tool, define what decisions the analytics should help you make.

I’d start with:

1. What do you need to learn?
   Examples: activation, retention, feature usage, conversion, errors, churn signals.

2. What events matter?
   Track a small set first, like:
   - `user_signed_up`
   - `onboarding_completed`
   - `project_created`
   - `feature_used`
   - `subscription_started`
   - `subscription_canceled`

3. What user properties are safe and useful?
   Avoid collecting sensitive data unless you truly need it. Prefer IDs, plan type, signup date, role, and coarse metadata.

4. What analytics product fits your needs?
   - Product analytics: PostHog, Amplitude, Mixpanel
   - Basic web analytics: Plausible, Fathom, Google Analytics
   - Warehouse-first: Segment, RudderStack, custom events into your DB/warehouse

5. How will you validate events?
   Add a tracking plan, test events in development, and make sure naming is consistent.

I’d recommend starting with a small event taxonomy and one analytics provider, then expanding only when you know which questions you cannot answer yet.
